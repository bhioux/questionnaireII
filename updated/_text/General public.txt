Public Instrument
The Federal University Oye-Ekiti, in collaboration with the National Research Fund, is conducting a study on
the inﬂuence of On-Air Personalities (OAPs) on the dissemination of falsehoods in the media and its
implications for national integration. Falsehoods refer to inaccurate or misleading information, including
rumours, fabricated content, or unveriﬁed claims, shared intentionally or unintentionally.
This questionnaire is intended for members of the general public who regularly engage with social media
content or listen to, watch, or follow broadcast and online media programmes.
Your participation is voluntary, and all information provided will be treated with strict conﬁdentiality.
